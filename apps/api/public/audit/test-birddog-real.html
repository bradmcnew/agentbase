Real-Time Sales Signals & Automated Research

BirdDog is an AI-powered sales research platform that automates prospect intelligence. Find the right accounts at the right time with buying signals unique to your business.

How BirdDog Works

BirdDog finds and monitors your accounts for signals that are unique to your company. When it finds a match, it pings you. Each day BirdDog monitors your CRM and net new accounts for important signals and shows you the best accounts.

Starting on the first day, all accounts get ranked so you know where to spend your time, and these rankings update as new signals are found.

Custom Signals

Create signals as yes/no questions. BirdDog hunts across public sources and returns matches with summaries, sources, and next steps.

Account Ranking

Every account gets scored and ranked from day one. Rankings update automatically as new signals are discovered across your pipeline.

Automated Plays

Create automated workflows that trigger actions when specific signals are found. Connect to your CRM, Slack, and email.

Daily Alerts

Get real-time alerts delivered to Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Email when BirdDog finds new signals on your accounts.

Account Recommendations

BirdDog surfaces high-intent, high-fit companies you aren't tracking yet based on lookalike analysis of your successful signals.

CRM Integration

Two-way sync with Salesforce and HubSpot. All data goes to your CRM automatically. CSV exports available for other platforms.
