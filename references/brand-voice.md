# AgentBase — Brand Voice & Keywords

> This is a living doc. Iterate as messaging gets validated through DMs and audits.

## Core Terms

| Term | Use for | Don't say |
|------|---------|-----------|
| agent-ready | The standard. "Is your product agent-ready?" | AI-ready, AI-optimized, agent-friendly |
| agent endpoint | The thing they claim. Technical, concrete. | AI endpoint, chatbot, widget |
| representative | What the endpoint IS. Their presence in the agent economy. | Assistant, bot, AI agent |
| agent economy | The market. Where agents interact with products. | AI ecosystem, AI landscape |
| agent audit | The test. How we prove the problem. | AI audit, readiness test, assessment |

## Tone Rules

1. State facts, don't pitch. "Agents are getting 3 things wrong about Resend" not "We can help you fix your agent presence"
2. Short sentences. "Agents are trying to interact with your product. There's nobody on the other end."
3. You're informing, not selling. You found something, you're sharing it.
4. No hype words. No "revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge," "unlock," "supercharge"
5. Engineer to engineer. Concrete nouns: endpoint, query, domain, audit. Not abstract: solution, platform, leverage.

## Key Lines

- **One-liner:** "Your representative in the agent economy."
- **Problem:** "Agents are trying to interact with your product. There's nobody on the other end."
- **CTA pattern:** "Is [company] agent-ready?" → audit proves they're not → "Claim your agent endpoint"
- **Landing page hook:** "AI agents need this to talk to your product"
